This policy applies to the Android apps Sudoku Grove, Lernspiele Kids, Lernspiele Teens, Venomous Snake, In Memoriam Pascal, LinguaFusion and The Knots of Alderwick ("the apps"). Visits to this website are covered by the website privacy policy (German). The German version of this policy is Datenschutzerklärung für die Apps.

2. In short

  • The apps collect no personal data and transmit no data to the developer or to third parties.
  • The apps have no internet permission. They contain no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no crash reporting, no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that send data, and no user accounts.
  • What you do in an app stays on your device.
  • The developer receives personal data in two cases only: order data from Google Play after a purchase (section 10) and your message if you contact support by email (section 11).

3. Data on your device

So that you can continue playing and learning, the apps store the following in the app's private storage on your device:

  • game progress and learning progress (for example solved tasks, results, statistics, review dates),
  • settings (for example theme, difficulty, read-aloud, speech rate),
  • profile names you enter in the app yourself, for example for several children in one family. A first name or nickname is enough.

The app sends this data nowhere. The developer has no access to it. Its only purpose is to provide the app's features. Where the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, GDPR) applies, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract for the use of the app).

To delete it: Android Settings > Apps > name of the app > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app. The developer holds no copy.

4. Android backup

Apps that use this feature include their progress in Android's automatic backup. If backup is switched on for your device, Android stores this data in your own Google account and restores it on a new device or after reinstalling. On Android 9 and later, Android encrypts the backup end to end with your device's screen lock (PIN, pattern or password) if one is set. The backup is a feature of Android and Google; the developer receives no data from it and has no access. Google's terms and privacy policy apply. You can switch backup off at any time in Android settings (depending on the device under "System" or "Google", entry "Backup").

5. Export, import and printing

Some apps can export progress, program code, a report or a puzzle booklet as a file. This happens only when you start it yourself: the app creates the file and opens the Android share sheet. Only you decide where the file goes (your storage, another app or a person); the app or person you choose is responsible for what happens next. On import, the app reads only the file you pick in the Android file picker, validates it and stores its content locally. For printing, the app opens the Android print dialog; you choose the printer or "Save as PDF".

6. Read-aloud

Apps with read-aloud pass the text to your device's text-to-speech engine. They use only voices that work locally on the device, so no text is sent to the internet for speech output. Read-aloud can be switched off in the app's settings.

7. Reminders (LinguaFusion)

LinguaFusion can remind you of reviews that are due. This feature stays off until you switch it on; Android then asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled and shown by the device, without a server and without a push service. You can switch them off in the app or in Android settings.

8. Permissions

The apps have no internet permission and request no access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or the advertising ID. The only permission an app may ask for is the notification permission in LinguaFusion, and only if you switch reminders on.

9. Differences between the apps

Sections 1 to 8 apply to all apps. In addition:

  • Sudoku Grove: read-aloud of hints and explanations; export and import of progress; printing of A4 puzzle booklets.
  • Lernspiele Kids: profiles with names you choose; read-aloud, on by default; parent report for printing or saving as PDF.
  • Lernspiele Teens: profiles with names you choose; read-aloud, off by default; export of learning progress as a file (for example for a teacher), only through the share sheet.
  • Venomous Snake and In Memoriam Pascal: your program code runs on the device (Python and Pascal respectively), without a server; export of code files through the share sheet.
  • LinguaFusion: pronunciation through read-aloud; reminders (section 7); export of learning progress.
  • The Knots of Alderwick: saved games on the device; export of saved games through the share sheet.

10. Purchase through Google Play

The apps are sold through Google Play. The seller and your contracting party for the purchase is Google; for buyers in the European Economic Area this is Google Commerce Limited (Ireland). Google processes your account and payment data as a controller in its own right, under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer receives no payment data such as card numbers.

For billing, tax, refunds and support, Google provides the developer with order data: order number, country, date, the app purchased and the price, in some cases also the buyer's name or email address. The developer uses this data only for accounting and to meet legal obligations.

  • Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (processing the purchase and the licence) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (tax record-keeping and retention obligations).
  • Retention: seven years under § 132(1) of the Austrian Federal Fiscal Code (Bundesabgabenordnung, BAO), counted from the end of the calendar year the records relate to, and beyond that only as long as they matter for pending tax proceedings. They are deleted afterwards.
  • Recipients: the data is disclosed only where the law requires it (for example to the tax authority) or where a tax adviser bound to confidentiality needs it for the accounts.

11. Support by email

If you write to peter@code-alongsi.de, the developer processes your email address, your name (if given) and the content of your message to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the request concerns a purchased app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering requests). The message is deleted once the request is settled and no legal retention obligation applies. Please do not send information about children that your question does not need.

12. Children

Lernspiele Kids, Lernspiele Teens, Sudoku Grove and LinguaFusion are also made for children and comply with the Google Play Families Policy. Venomous Snake, In Memoriam Pascal and The Knots of Alderwick are made for teenagers aged 13 and over and for adults. No app collects or transmits data from children: there are no ads, no advertising ID, no location data, no chat, no exchange between users and no links to purchase or donation pages. Profile names stay on the device (section 3).

13. Your rights

Under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. As the developer holds no data about you apart from order data and support messages, these rights in practice concern that data. You manage the data on your device yourself (section 3). Please send requests to peter@code-alongsi.de. The developer makes no automated decisions and does no profiling.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). In Austria this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40-42, 1030 Vienna, phone +43 1 52 152-0, email dsb@dsb.gv.at, www.dsb.gv.at.

14. Changes

If an app's data processing changes, for example through a new feature, this policy is updated before the changed version is released.
